How to play:
  • A coffee cup slides in from the side. Drop it onto the tower.
  • Any overhang gets sliced off — the cup shrinks!
  • Perfect center drops give bonus points and build your combo.
  • Miss completely and the tower collapses. Game over!
  • Speed increases every 5 cups. How high can you stack?
